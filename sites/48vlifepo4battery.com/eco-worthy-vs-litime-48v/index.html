Where they meet: the 100Ah / 5.12kWh class

The overlap class is 48V 100Ah — 5,120Wh, the standard storage brick. Eco-Worthy fields it as a single rack module with inverter communications. LiTime fields it nine ways in this catalog: rack, floor, marine, Bluetooth and Victron-ready variants at several price tiers. Same stored energy in every one of those rows, so the spread between them is the price of format and features, stated in $/Wh instead of adjectives.

  • Rack format buys CAN/RS485 closed-loop inverter pairing and one-module-at-a-time expansion — the home-storage default.
  • Floor format buys simpler placement where no rack exists, often at a small $/Wh premium.
  • The 314Ah outlier: Eco-Worthy's big floor battery undercuts every 100Ah row on $/Wh because one case and one BMS carry ~16kWh — the trade is a very heavy install and an all-or-nothing failure mode, detailed on the 314Ah page.

One caution on the kit row: Eco-Worthy's home-backup bundle prices a battery and an inverter together, so its $/Wh reads high against bare batteries. It is in the table because the maker sells it that way — compare it against a battery-plus-inverter total, not against bare-battery rows.

Frequently asked questions

Is Eco-Worthy or LiTime cheaper at 48V?

By $/Wh, the current leader across both catalogs is the ECO-WORTHY PowerMega 314 | 51.2V 314AH Lithium Battery 16kWh | Active Balance | UL1973 — 1 Pack at $2,400 ($0.150/Wh). In the shared 100Ah class the brands sit within a few cents per watt-hour of each other and maker sales move the lead — the live table, refreshed several times a day, is the standing answer.

Which brand is better for a 48V golf cart conversion?

In the catalogs tracked here, LiTime is the golf-cart brand: it fields GC2-format 30Ah/60Ah packs, cart-specific 100Ah units and a complete conversion kit, while Eco-Worthy's tracked 48V line is storage-focused. Whichever pack you choose, its continuous discharge rating must cover your controller's draw, and it needs a 58.4V LiFePO4-profile charger.

Can I parallel Eco-Worthy and LiTime 48V batteries together?

Makers advise against it: match brand, model and age in a parallel bank, and closed-loop inverter communication needs one BMS protocol in charge. Build the bank from a single model — the per-module $/Wh saving compounds with every module you add.

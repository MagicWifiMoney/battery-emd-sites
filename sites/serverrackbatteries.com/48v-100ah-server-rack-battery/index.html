Why not just buy the cheapest sticker price here?

These models store different amounts of energy — from 5,120Wh to 20,480Wh — so the sticker alone misleads. $/Wh normalizes for size: it is the price of one watt-hour of storage, whichever case it comes in.

Is a 48V 100Ah server rack battery the same as a 51.2V 100Ah battery?

Yes — same battery, two labels. LiFePO4 cells are 3.2V nominal, so a 16-cell '48V' pack is 51.2V nominal and stores 5,120Wh at 100Ah. Makers title the identical class both ways; this page tracks them together.

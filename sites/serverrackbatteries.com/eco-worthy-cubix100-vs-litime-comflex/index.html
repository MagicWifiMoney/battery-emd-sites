What separates three identical-capacity modules?

  • Price per Wh: the headline. On today's numbers the spread between the cheapest and priciest of the three is roughly $240 for identical stored energy — money that buys a second module's worth of progress every fourth purchase.
  • Heating: SunGoldPower's self-heating variant (the SGH48100T row in the table) suits unheated garages and sheds; the Cubix100 and base SG48100P assume a conditioned space or a warm climate.
  • Communications: all three brands publish inverter compatibility lists (CAN/RS485). The deciding check is your exact inverter model on the maker's list — see our inverter compatibility guide.
  • Multi-pack rows: SunGoldPower sells 2- and 4-module bundles in the same feed; their per-unit math differs from the single-module rows, so compare singles to singles.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, the Eco-Worthy Cubix100 or the LiTime ComFlex?

On live prices today: the Cubix100 is $860 ($0.168/Wh) and the ComFlex is $1,100 ($0.215/Wh), with SunGoldPower's SG48100P between them at $981. All three store 5.12kWh, so $/Wh is the complete price story; features and inverter lists are the tiebreakers.

Can I parallel a Cubix100 with a ComFlex in one bank?

Makers advise matching brand, model and age in a parallel bank — different BMS units share current unevenly and closed-loop communication needs one protocol master. Build the bank from one module type; the table's per-module saving compounds with every module you add.

Do these rack batteries include the rack cabinet?

Typically not — the module price is the battery in its rack-mount case; cabinets, busbars and cables are separate line items. Factor them into a bank build (the 10kWh bank page on this site lists what else a build needs).

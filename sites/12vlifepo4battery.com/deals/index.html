How drops will be measured

Each refresh of this tracker stores a price snapshot. A model qualifies for this page when the same listing is available now and its price has fallen at least 5% and at least $25 against the oldest snapshot at least 6 days old. Rankings are by percentage drop; the table will show the old and new prices and the $/Wh then and now, with the snapshot dates. A few hand-tracked models (for example Vatrer) have no snapshot history and will never appear here.

Until the first edition lands (expected around November 28, 2026 plus a few days), the live price table is the current picture.

How prices are gathered: automatically from the manufacturers' own online stores, refreshed several times a day. We compute price per watt-hour from each battery's rated capacity. We never invent ratings or discounts — a struck-through price appears only when the maker's own store lists one. Full method on the about page.

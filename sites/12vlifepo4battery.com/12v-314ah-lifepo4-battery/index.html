The 314Ah class is where 12V stored energy gets cheapest: roughly 4,019Wh in one battery, routinely the lowest $/Wh in the entire 12V catalog. If your space fits the larger case, this is the value play for off-grid and big RV banks. Wiring smaller packs to the same energy instead follows the series-parallel guide; panel ranges for a 4kWh bank are on the solar page, and charge profiles for a bank this size are in the charging guide.

One caution: a single 314Ah battery is a single point of failure and a heavy lift (around 60–70 lb). For mission-critical setups, two smaller packs in parallel add redundancy. This is also the class where Eco-Worthy's value line bottoms out — Eco-Worthy vs LiTime prices that fight.

Four models vs forty-five: what depth actually buys

Eco-Worthy's 12V line, as tracked here, is two bets: a 100Ah pack (1,280Wh — the RV, marine and small-solar staple) and a 314Ah pack (4,019Wh — the big single-case value play), each in two case variants. That is the whole catalog: 4 rows. If your job is one of those two sizes, Eco-Worthy is in the fight; if it isn't, the brand simply doesn't sell you a battery.

LiTime's line is the opposite strategy: 45 tracked 12V rows spanning small fish-finder packs, a full 100Ah feature ladder (bare, Bluetooth, low-temperature and self-heating tiers), 200Ah and 300Ah-plus classes, and trolling-motor and RV specials. Depth means the exact tier you need exists — and that some of those tiers carry $/Wh figures a two-size catalog never has to defend. Read LiTime's rows by capacity class, not by brand average: its cheapest-per-Wh rows and its smallest packs sit at opposite ends of this table.

Frequently asked questions

Is Eco-Worthy or LiTime cheaper for 12V LiFePO4?

On live prices, the cheapest stored energy in this head-to-head is the ECO-WORTHY EnergyTrek 12V 314Ah LiFePO4 Lithium Battery with SOC LEDs and Low-temp Protection at $449 ($0.112/Wh) — an Eco-Worthy 314Ah model. In the shared 100Ah class the brands price closely and the lead changes with maker sales, which is why the table above refreshes several times a day instead of declaring a permanent winner.

Why does LiTime have so many more 12V models than Eco-Worthy?

Different strategies: LiTime builds a variant for every feature tier and niche (Bluetooth, low-temperature, self-heating, trolling, small packs), while Eco-Worthy's tracked 12V catalog concentrates on 100Ah and 314Ah. Depth helps when you need a specific feature or size; it does not by itself lower the $/Wh of the classes both brands sell.

Can I mix Eco-Worthy and LiTime batteries in one 12V bank?

Makers advise against mixing brands, models or ages in a series/parallel bank — BMS behavior and internal resistance differ, and the weakest unit limits the string. Pick one brand per bank; the table prices that commitment class by class.
